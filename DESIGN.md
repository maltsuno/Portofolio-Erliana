## Brand & Style

This design system translates creative vitality into an elevated, literary monograph aesthetic. Tailored for creative practitioners and cultural design portfolios, it blends graceful classical typography with contemporary utility. It avoids corporate sterility and excessive digital embellishment in favor of an artful print-inspired layout reminiscent of indie design monographs, editorial art books, and curated gallery catalogues.

The voice is articulate, cultured, and thoughtfully self-assured. The emotional tone projects intellectual rigor balanced with warmth and creative spark. The visual style pairs classical Renaissance serif architecture with crisp modern grotesque details, generous gallery-like margins, and precise ambient accents.

## Colors

The palette grounds itself in high-contrast print logic. The canvas is dominated by `editorial-paper` (`#F2F4F3`) and pure `monograph-white` (`#FFFFFF`), avoiding pure digital greys to simulate natural heavy-stock book paper.

- **Primary (`#123BBA` / `electric-cobalt`)**: The intellectual anchor. Used for primary typographic headlines, authoritative badges, key navigational structures, and primary interactive states.
- **Secondary (`#B7F000` / `acid-lime`)**: A sharp editorial accent. Reserved for micro-highlights, bookmark tags, starbursts, and attention moments that punctuate the literary flow.
- **Tertiary (`#E94B3C` / `vermilion-coral`)**: An expressive art-direction note. Deployed for monograph labels, edition tags, status badges, and critical inline callouts.
- **Neutral (`#F2F4F3` / `editorial-paper`)**: The underlying page surface, providing an organic, non-glare foundation.

Text hierarchy relies strictly on `electric-cobalt` for high-contrast titles and deep headers, while `ink-secondary` (`#40527A`) handles supporting editorial captions, project metadata, and annotations to preserve clear tonal separation.

## Typography

The typographical pairing forms the core of the monograph aesthetic. 

- **EB Garamond** anchors all major titles, chapter intros, and prominent quotes. Its historical proportions and delicate terminals provide intellectual sophistication without becoming dry or archaic. Headline tracking remains tight to reinforce an editorial, poster-like density.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** acts as the modern partner for body text, interface metadata, captions, and structural labels. It balances Garamond’s romantic curvature with clean, open apertures and high legibility at micro scales.

Headlines larger than 32px automatically transition to mobile-specific variants on smaller displays to preserve harmonious line breaks and vertical flow.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows an asymmetrical 12-column editorial grid. Rather than centering everything neutrally, compositions utilize wide catalogue margins, dramatic tension between text blocks, and varied column spans (such as 7/5 or 8/4 splits).

- **Desktop (1024px and above)**: Max-width fixed at 1200px (75rem) with 48px lateral page padding. Section separation spans generous intervals of `space-3xl` (96px) to `space-4xl` (128px), giving project case studies an unhurried, exhibition-like cadence.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: Two-column rhythm with 32px margins and compact headline ratios.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: Fluid single-column layout with 20px edge margins. Vertical section intervals contract to 64px to keep navigation direct while retaining atmospheric breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids synthetic depth, heavy multi-stage drop shadows, and glassmorphism. It models depth after physical sheets of fine paper layered upon a drafting table:

- **Flat Planes**: Visual boundaries are primarily created by tonal juxtaposition—`monograph-white` cards set atop the `editorial-paper` base canvas, demarcated by fine hairline dividers (`1px solid #DDE2E1`).
- **Subtle Cobalt Diffusion**: When a surface warrants physical lifting (such as hovering project plates or floating navigation rails), use an ambient shadow tinted with the primary blue rather than a dead grey: `0 4px 16px rgba(18, 59, 186, 0.08)`.
- **Interactive State Elevation**: Hovering on cards slightly translates the element `-2px` on the Y-axis and deepens the blue-tinted shadow to `0 8px 24px rgba(18, 59, 186, 0.12)` with a 200ms ease-out curve.

## Shapes

The design uses a Level 2 curvature system (`0.5rem` base, scaling to `1rem` on container cards and `1.5rem` on prominent feature blocks). 

- **Containers & Cards**: Standardized at `rounded-lg` (16px / 1rem). This radius softens mechanical rectangles into tactile, archival book cards.
- **Buttons, Badges & Stickers**: May utilize full pill radii (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to contrast against the structured editorial grid.
- **Doodles & Starbursts**: Hand-drawn flourishes and typographic asterisks maintain clean mathematical vector geometry with slightly rounded vertices to blend with the structural serif display.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `electric-cobalt` (`#123BBA`) background with white typography (`Plus Jakarta Sans`, 14px bold), `rounded-full` contour, and 12px 24px padding. Hover introduces a 1.02 scale transformation.
- **Secondary / Outlined**: Transparent background with a 1.5px border in `electric-cobalt`, displaying cobalt text. Invert on hover.
- **Micro / Action Links**: Text link styled with an inline rightward directional arrow (`→`) that shifts 4px on hover.

### Cards & Project Folios
- Built on `monograph-white` with a `16px` border radius and `1px solid #DDE2E1`.
- Projects feature an edge-to-edge photography frame at top, followed by EB Garamond title, small metadata row in `ink-secondary`, and category badge.
- Cards maintain an internal padding of `space-md` (24px) for content wells.

### Chips & Stickers
- **Metadata Chips**: Low-profile capsules using `Plus Jakarta Sans` 12px uppercase, 7px 14px padding, and neutral grey-blue borders.
- **Sticker Accents**: Distinctive graphic elements rendered in `acid-lime` (`#B7F000`) or `vermilion-coral` (`#E94B3C`) with cobalt or white text. Used sparingly to mark awards, current availability, or specialized skills.

### Form Inputs
- `monograph-white` background, 1px border using `#DDE2E1`, rounded to 8px (`rounded-sm`).
- Focus rings display a 2px offset border in `electric-cobalt`.
- Labels must consistently render in uppercase `Plus Jakarta Sans` with `0.08em` letter spacing above the input target.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- 20px squares and circles with a 1.5px border in `ink-secondary`.
- Active state fills with `electric-cobalt` containing a sharp white vector indicator.

### Archival Lists & Timeline
- Separated by thin `1px solid #DDE2E1` horizontal rules.
- Columnar layout featuring year marker in EB Garamond serif on the left column, role and narrative body on the right.
